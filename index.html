SnapRedact: Local Browser Screenshots With Auto-Redaction and Searchable PDF Export

SnapRedact is not built yet. If Edge's free Web Capture is already enough for you, skip this — the registration below asks exactly that, and honest "it's enough" answers count against building it. But if you regularly share screenshots and first have to hunt down and blur emails, phone numbers, names, amounts or order numbers by hand, this is the gap being tested: a planned extension that captures the page locally and masks those details in one click, for an indicative $19 one-time. We will only build it if 20 people register before November 10, 2026.

Why this shape

  • Screenshot tools are not scarce: GoFullPage reports roughly 11 million Chrome users and Awesome Screenshot roughly 3–4 million. In those products, redaction/masking, PDF export and higher resolution sit behind paid tiers (GoFullPage Premium is about $12/year; Awesome Screenshot premium starts around $36/year) — bundled into all-in-one subscriptions. There is no small one-time-purchase tool that does only the "mask before sharing" job.
  • The honest counter-argument is built into this test: Edge already ships Web Capture for free, with full-page capture and basic annotation. If that covers your needs, a paid redaction tool has no reason to exist — which is why one registration question asks directly whether the built-in tool is enough for you.
  • Everything would run in your own browser: no account, no screenshot ever uploaded to a server. Auto-detection would start with pattern-based rules (emails, phone formats, order-number shapes) plus manual boxes for anything it misses — it will not be perfect, and the page does not pretend otherwise.

Planned capabilities (none of this exists yet)

  • Full-page and region capture inside the browser, fully local
  • One-click detection and masking (blur or solid block) of emails, phone numbers, names, amounts and order numbers, with manual add/remove boxes
  • Minimal annotation: arrows, rectangles, short text notes
  • Export as PNG or searchable PDF
  • No account, no uploads, no cloud processing of your screenshots

This page promises nothing that is not written above. Features, if the product is built, will follow what registrants actually ask for.

Who this is for

  • Support, operations, QA and finance staff who send screenshots to customers, colleagues or public channels
  • Anyone who posts screenshots publicly (bug reports, tutorials, social posts) and has to scrub private details first
  • If your browser's built-in capture already does everything you need, this is not for you — please still consider answering, because that answer decides whether this gets built

Questions

Can I use it now?
No. Nothing has been built; this page is the whole thing.

Does registering mean I have to buy it?
No. It only means "notify me if it launches". Decide when there is a real product to look at.

Why not just use the browser's built-in capture?
You should, if it is enough. It captures and annotates for free; what it does not do is find and mask sensitive details for you. This validation exists to find out whether that one difference matters to at least 20 people.

Will auto-redaction catch everything?
No detector catches everything. The plan is pattern-based detection plus quick manual boxes, and the exported image is yours to check before sending — the tool assists, it does not guarantee.
